💡 Korean Age System Guide

  • • International Age (만 나이): Officially unified in Korea on June 28, 2023. Starts at 0, adds 1 on each birthday. Used for all legal, medical, and administrative matters.
  • • Traditional Korean Age (세는나이): Starts at 1 at birth, adds 1 every January 1st. Widely used in social hierarchy, friendship, and casual conversations.
  • • Calendar Year Age (연 나이): Current Year minus Birth Year. Applied to selective Korean laws including the Juvenile Protection Act and Military Service Act.
